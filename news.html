AkimiTech CAD: Precision Drafting Without the Paper

Our flagship engineering tool has hit version 13. Built specifically for local hardware constraints, this lightweight Computer-Aided Design suite puts professional drafting power into the hands of engineers, architects, and draftsmen. Design 2D floor plans, structural drawings, and schematics entirely on-screen — no plotter paper, no expensive mistakes, no waiting for a printing shop.

Real-World Impact
  • 60% faster iterations — no more redrawing by hand when a client requests a change.
  • Cuts printing overhead — every revision lives in the digital file, eliminating wasteful paper plots.
  • Instant collaboration — export and share designs via email or WhatsApp.
  • Automated precision — dimensions and layers are checked automatically, reducing costly on-site errors.
