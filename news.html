AkimiTech CAD — Drawings Without the Paper

A lightweight Computer-Aided Design tool that puts professional drafting power into the hands of engineers, architects, and draftsmen in Juba and beyond. Design 2D floor plans, structural drawings, and schematics entirely on-screen — no plotter paper, no expensive mistakes, no waiting for a printing shop.

How it changes the working environment
  • 60% faster drawings — no more redrawing by hand when a client requests a change
  • Cuts printing costs — every revision lives in the file, not on a new sheet of paper
  • Instant sharing — send a design by email or WhatsApp instead of hand-delivering copies
  • Fewer costly errors — dimensions and layers are checked automatically
  • Opens the door for young engineers — learn modern CAD without needing an expensive workstation
